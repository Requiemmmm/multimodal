\begin{abstract}
We study modality and content control in early-fusion multimodal models with a shared vocabulary. Continuous suffixes expose an internal control direction, while ordinary text-token suffixes test whether that direction survives the native interface. We separate image routing, image validity, and target-content control and analyze the gap between them.
\end{abstract}
